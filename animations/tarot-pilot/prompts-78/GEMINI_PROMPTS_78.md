```text
Create ONE complete tarot card illustration for the MaeMorKratai deck.

REFERENCE PRIORITY
Use the attached IMG_5392.JPG as the primary reference for the mascot identity and drawing style. If an approved card is also attached, use that second image only to match the deck's frame, spacing, palette and level of detail. Create the new scene below; do not copy the reference pose or its printed card.

CHARACTER IDENTITY
Preserve the ivory-white rabbit's oversized rounded head, soft upright ears with pale pink details, tiny dark oval eyes, coral-red round nose with small ivory highlights, simple curved mouth, plump body, rounded paws and peach-orange / muted green scarf. Change its pose as the scene requires. All companion rabbits must use the same visual language, with simple scarf or ear variations. Keep anatomy clear: two ears, two front paws and two hind paws per rabbit, with natural occlusion, no duplicated limbs and no human fingers. Keep the full main character within the card.

ART STYLE
Match the reference's simple hand-drawn 2D shapes, flat matte colors, gently imperfect edges, minimal pale pink shading and warm, charming expression. Deep purple is the unifying background color. Use ivory, pale pink, peach-orange, muted green, lavender and small warm yellow accents. Keep the illustration readable as a small card. Avoid photorealism, 3D, realistic fur, glossy plastic, cinematic lighting, heavy black outlines, anime eyes, elaborate costume and Halloween hats. Use only modest role accessories explicitly requested below.

CARD LAYOUT
Portrait format, target aspect ratio 5:8. One flat front-facing illustrated card filling the canvas, not a product mockup, collage, contact sheet or photographed object. Use one thin ivory rounded frame, inset about 5% from the edges. Keep ears, paws and important symbols inside a 7% safe margin. Leave the bottom 12% visually quiet so the website can add a title later. No words, letters, printed numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks. Keep the camera framing and border consistent across the deck.

SYMBOL CLARITY
Follow the exact object counts in the scene. Keep counted objects distinct and visible, with no extra suit symbols hidden in decorations or the frame. Pentacle coins each contain one simple five-point star inside a circle. Wands are wooden with a few small green leaves; cups are rounded ivory goblets; swords are simple silver-gray fantasy blades with warm yellow hilts. Keep blades away from bodies. Preserve foreground, character and background separation for later gentle animation.
```
